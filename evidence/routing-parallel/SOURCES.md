## R01 — Routing and shedding are not interchangeable types

Main `sections/06_field.tex`, `subsec:shedding`, defines shedding as the
direction-neutral surplus process. Its native pressure specialization uses
`Delta_close = P^SADAR_W - C_close` and `X_shedding = max(0, Delta_close)` at
`eq:closure-residual` and `eq:shedding-excess`. A negative residual is
deficit/open, not a positive sheddic result.

`eq:shedding-route-partition` allows endoshedding, exoshedding, redirected
support and open loss only after declaring the sheddic path. General motion
between coarse cells, CPU work scheduling and kernel selection are not thereby
shedding. The user wording can name this application's surplus routing; it
does not erase the source distinction between an arbitrary route and a route
carrying a computed surplus.

**Disposition: supported, source fidelity.** A routing output must identify
whether it is support motion, a declared surplus placement, or an admitted
sheddic path. A count-to-load example is a model bridge, not an automatic
identification of flip count with SADAR pressure.

## R02 — Surplus arithmetic alone does not supply the route witness

Main `eq:fold-outer-closure` requires exact inner identities, certificates and
scope maps to be bound by an independently certified outer construction.
Attaching an unrelated higher-scale loop does not establish that fold.
`eq:expose-inner-lineage` preserves surviving inner orientation, history and
certificates; inner beats cannot be reused as outer returns.

Main `eq:sheddic-path` names retained forms through E2 or C6/C8/C10 and higher
even enclosures. `subsec:sheddic-path-audit` rejects a positive-surplus claim
without its declared form or with violated retention condition. E2 is the
Duon core (`subsec:e2-duon`); two atomic traversals do not by themselves admit
that motif. `eq:support-enclosure-retention` keeps TTL distinct from RCD duration
and requires compatible available retention and observation windows.

The vendored `validate_shedding` checks the folded outer binding, retained
lineages/certificates, strict source/outer/route commitment order, exact
load/cost/status, route/target and digest bindings. It does not by itself check
the full Main sheddic-form/TTL audit. Callers must keep that additional scope
visible. It also cannot authenticate omitted source-event or model-bridge
payloads merely from 64-hex digest strings; an application wrapper must retain
those payloads and recompute their bindings.

**Disposition: conditional, implementation contract.** A `SheddingRecord` is
usable for the producer's computed-surplus/route contract, but not a universal
proof of all sheddic-form or physical routing obligations.

## R03 — Existing Manual example permits an explicit load bridge

Manual `eq:awareness-shedding-bridge` declares a toy load `L=C_cap+J` and cost
`C_close=C_cap`. It explicitly does not rename `J` as SADAR pressure. A positive
record binds source state/event, outer certificate, operands, route, target,
order and digest before exoshedding exposure. Zero means no shedding; negative
means deficit/open. Its fold/shedding execution preserves both inner waves.

Matter I `manual2:declared-recurrence` separately computes signed response,
capacity residuals and excesses; its later retention/exposure bridge is
explicitly model-specific. Routed-material reclosure or radiation needs a
further construction and observation map.

The new collision application may similarly declare a count-to-load bridge
before evaluation. One minimal falsifiable choice is `L=F_A`, the retained
number of A's flips in the completed window, and `C=1`, in a named dimensionless
count-derived load coordinate. This is a proposed downstream choice, not an
extracted pressure law or a physical energy balance. A total-pair or
contact-weighted bridge would be a different declaration.

## R06 — Beats, owner octave naming and wavelength

Main `eq:beat-reclosure` and `eq:cumulative-closure-counts` define N distinct
certified compatible closures as N complete closure cycles and N−1 reclosure
beats from the lineage origin. `eq:closure-phase-window` handles an arbitrary
window without subtracting another origin. Ordered positive completion
separations are cadence and may be nonuniform. `eq:beat-count` in the field
section is explicitly a support multiplicity formula, not a count of beats.

The actual compiler's Python and C++ `beats` implementations return
`max(0, phases-1)` after trace validation; `rate` returns closure count divided
by the positive event span. The current short replay has two initial returns
plus eight updates, hence ten closures and nine beats for each carrier. Its
host completion separations are ten native indices; its short return has two
atomic traversals. These are different typed quantities. The Dec replay's
three explicit events per return are also distinct from the host's sparse
native-cut encoding.

The owner clarified that “octaves” here means the octal coordinate in Main,
not an alias for beat count. The initial proposed alias was therefore removed
before the final implementation. Searching only the literal word `octave` had
missed the relevant retained-address construction. Main
`sections/03_afc_basis.tex`, `eq:first-anchor`, gives `00_8` and the finite octal
retained-address domain, and explicitly distinguishes address, Q4 vertex,
fibre zero, Null and scope. `eq:closure-trace` retains the start and ordered
events; the return witness supplies the closure relation rather than endpoint
equality alone.

The application now declares an explicit finite map: scope/scale/lineage and
trace-digest namespace, plus base-8 encoding of the committed native cut, is
bound to each immutable event ID. Event ordinal, Q4 vertex/binary bits, fibre
role and beat count remain separate fields. This is a declared representation
of retained event addresses, not a source-derived unique numerical encoding or
a claim that a Q4 vertex is the octal address. Start and terminal event
addresses differ even when their projected vertices coincide; the witness
still binds the terminal event to its retained start. Beat records link actual
reclosures to their completion event/address. No 2:1 frequency relation,
eight-event rule or fold-scale octave is introduced. The source cadence-ratio
constructor separately requires its actual repeated-contact protocol and
positive denominator; similar update rates are not that witness.

Wavelength rows in Manual `manual:hydrogen:energy-transitions` are downstream
external comparisons: a cited vacuum wavelength supplies `f=c/lambda` and
external energy. Inverse-wavelength target ratios are not native path lengths.
The current topology carries no metric length and no spatial phase-propagation
map. A closed route's traversal count is not automatically its wavelength, and
its zero net displacement does not establish zero wavelength. An SI wavelength
claim needs the actual spatial/phase observation law, length/clock or velocity
calibration, compatible medium and units, and comparison uncertainty.

**Disposition: supported for closure/beat/cadence counts and source octal
address distinctions; declared application encoding for numeric address rows;
missing premise for a physical wavelength.**

## R07 — Selected implementation contract

The parent selected the total-pair bridge `L=F_A+F_B`, `C=1`, before the final
study. It replaces the earlier one-carrier feasibility choice as the actual
application contract; neither choice is asserted to be a native pressure law.
Each original carrier's two formation returns and eight selected returns map
to a separately admitted recurrent `3:3` support. Every support return retains
three distinct prior Monon components, three ordered blocks referencing that
triple, and the retained shell. This follows the p:q construction convention
in Matter I `matter1_campaign_engine.py:265`; it is not a three-outbound,
three-return traversal claim. “Proton-facing” does not instantiate a physical
incoming-proton state.

The implementation constructs the positive route's two-return Duon/E2 support
before the independent outer fold and commits its surplus record afterward.
Seat compatibility, available retention and required window are explicit exact
model operands. Their gate requires `available >= required > 0`; it does not
derive retention from cadence or RCD. The single active route carries X, and
the other partition channels carry zero. The implemented routes are redirected
support and exoshedding; the latter also exercises the producer's bound inner
exposure check. Endoshedding and open-loss amounts remain zero in this fixture.

The wrapper retains full event and bridge payloads and validates them by exact
reconstruction, along with all higher supports, form, outer witness, partition,
octal address bindings and observables. Route configurations receive separate
outer continuation identities, preventing changed construction times from
reusing an immutable outer event ID. The two materialized formation returns
remain shared under an intervention that preserves their actual source prefix.

## Disposition and next focused tasks

Source support exists for a declared surplus-routing extension with preserved
histories and explicit formation/route/retention records. The load bridge and
route selection remain application model choices. Parallel CPU/GPU execution
must reproduce those fixed inputs and outputs; trace retention and thread/work
ordering must not alter the model's sampled realization. Device throughput is
neither a native clock nor physical collision accuracy.

Not checked here: the new CPU/GPU implementation, its numerical ranges or
performance; final route-module correctness; full upstream manuscript validity;
physical incoming-proton state, scattering interaction, outgoing-particle map,
cross section, energy/momentum or SI wavelength calibration. These missing
premises hold only the dependent physical claims.
