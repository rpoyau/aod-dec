## Conditional finite-state sufficiency

not describe admissible histories or have a valid next update.

The sufficient outputs are the two inventories' integer group counts, selected
word/hand/token rank, completion/contact indicators, retained value/presence and
next compact key. The key alone is not claimed to reconstruct historical event
IDs, authenticate witnesses or address a particular relation by identity. Those
operations use the separately preserved immutable archive. No universal
minimality, global 256/512-state count or unbounded stationary-law claim is made.

The commuting update follows by the following finite cases:

1. Each field's bound formation contributes one token of each hand. Each active
   relation contributes exactly `abs(r)` tokens, all with its recorded sign.
   Its separate age slot and presence bit determine eligibility. Therefore the

2. An eligible recipe depends only on the incoming axis and the fixed input mask.
   It uses three distinct axes, avoids the incoming axis on its first edge and

3. The common exact ticket and ordered finite inventory determine the selected

   A completion increments its actual target-return count and since-contact

4. Both completions create contact; otherwise none is manufactured. Actual
   since-contact hinge lists have the newly incremented `since` cardinalities.

   its recorded reflection counts supply the previous list cardinalities. The

## Exact probabilities and atomic pushforwards

With fewer than three open axes no recipe exists. With three open axes there
are four words if the incoming axis is open and six otherwise. With four open
axes there are eighteen. Hence `G` belongs to `{0,4,6,18}`. Within six attempts,
`3 <= count_a <= 9`, `2 <= count_b <= 8` and `abs(delta) <= 7`; clipping gives
`abs(r) <= 42`. Each field has between two and 86 opportunities per word.

Every positive inventory total is `Z=G*n`, with `n` an integer from two through
86. Since `G` divides 36 and `n` divides `lcm(1,...,86)`, every such total divides
`D=36*lcm(1,...,86)`. Uniform integer tickets in `[0,D)` partition into exactly
`D/Z` tickets per elementary opportunity. There is no rounding of fractional
percentages and no finite-grid approximation. Matched interventions retain the
same ticket even when their totals differ.

For each executed vertex prefix, `atomic_prefix_counts` pushes the remaining
complete opportunity inventory onto its next H1 edge. Dividing by its positive
total gives conditional next-edge probabilities. The unconditioned edge chain
recovers the geometric-word marginal, not the full handed-word law. Let n_h be
the number of tokens per word for hand h, and n=n_minus+n_plus. Choose and retain
hand with `P(h)=n_h/n`; conditioned on that hand, the successive edge ratios
telescope to `P(w|h)=1/G`. Therefore `P(h,w)=n_h/(G*n)`. A uniform rank among the
n_h tokens gives `P(h,w,token)=1/(G*n)=1/Z`. Initially n_minus=n_plus=1 and G=18,
so the geometric-word probability is 1/18 while each handed-word probability is
1/36. The edge projection alone does not select or preserve the hand/token label.

Mandatory hinge is inserted at the declared turn as its separate event type.
Tests compare the edge counts against direct expanded opportunity enumeration,
including complete and empty prefixes. The unchanged implementation consumes one
macro ticket to select the full hand/word/token inventory. The factorization
above describes that joint law; it does not claim independently resampled atomic
choices or a combined H1/fibre kernel.
