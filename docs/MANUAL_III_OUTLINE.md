# Proposed Manual III: D.E.C. computation

This outline is a proposal for later Manual III authoring. It introduces no new
Main definitions and makes no upstream manuscript or publication change.

1. **Declared computation.** Bind the distinction/carrier, admitted objects,
   kernel, preparation, exact quantity domains and update order. Separate the
   execution engine from application laws and observation maps.
2. **Committed traces.** Retained starts, immutable identities, parents,
   branch/hinge/counterbranch, complete return certificates and deterministic
   replay. Separate serialization order from physical time.
3. **Detection.** Candidate/verified/rejected/unresolved returns; recurrence and
   reclosure beats; independent outer closure; physical shedding, routes,
   receipts and unresolved current projections. Infer support signatures from
   component structure and scoped window evidence without expected motif labels:
   `1:2:x`, `3:j:k` and Duon construction. Track Duad seating, coupling and
   shedding as independent, evidence-bound state/relation axes.
4. **Scoped views.** Octal retained coordinate, source-bound fusion family,
   spatial/support span, observation/acquisition/comparison windows and duration
   participation. Left memory, right censoring and non-additive alternatives.
5. **Boundary computation.** Oriented current, internal cancellation, shells,
   disjoint partitions and the shared moving sweep. Distinguish present boundary
   equality from a sufficient reduced update state.
6. **Finite reductions.** Exact finite p/P example, lumpability and paired
   trajectories; recurrence/coupling reuse conditions and concrete counterexamples.
7. **Bounded resources.** Active model, resident cache and durable history;
   witness retrieval, checkpoints, failures, incomplete evidence and measured
   high-water scopes. Execution budgets are not physical capacities.
8. **Observation and errors.** Input and output domains, SI anchoring, shared-input
   dependencies, exact ratios, tau angular representation and declared geometric
   approximations. Preserve unknown uncertainty.
9. **Applications.** Closure/fusion/coupling examples, maser apparatus,
   Earth–water–Moon current, proton pair and electron–wire. The Tau search starts
   with collision/cavity/sensor input and classifies the resulting trace;
   candidate formation is an engine output. Keep generation coverage, detector
   coverage and historical score reproduction separate. Physical orbital
   trajectories likewise require executed evolution and observation bindings.
10. **Execution profiles.** Actual reference, AOT and JIT operations, passive
    tracing overhead, compilation/loading/warmup and total cost. Include failed,
    censored and unavailable runs with the source-bound acceptance record.

Reusable material currently lives in `docs/TRACE_PROFILE.md`,
`docs/trace-profile.typ`, and the executable examples and checks delivered with
this D.E.C. implementation. Main retains the definitions and proofs; the future
Manual III consumes their exact labels and source versions.
